# What is a Lab?

### **Learn, then teach**

---

## The Learning Loop

1. **Search**
2. **Read**
3. **Think**
4. **Questions**
5. **Understand**

---

## 1. Search

- Gather **multiple sources**
- Prioritize **reliable & recent** information
- Build a solid baseline

---

## 2. Read

- **Take notes** actively
- Rephrase in your **own words**
- **Flag unclear parts** for later review

---

## 3. Think

- **Connect** new concepts to existing ideas
- Ground theories with a **concrete example**
- **Visualize** relationships (draw a diagram)

---

## 4. Questions

- Test the edges: **Where does it break?**
- Treat every gap as your **next topic**
- **Loop back** to step 1 as needed

---

## 5. Understand

- Explain it simply — **no jargon**
- **Teach it** to someone else
- **Refine** your mental model from feedback

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Learn, then teach:** The ultimate feedback loop
- **Five steps, one cycle:** Continuous improvement
- **Gaps guide learning:** Questions drive focus
- **Teaching proves mastery:** If you can’t explain it, re-study
